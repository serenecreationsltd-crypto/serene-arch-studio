The Uganda building approval journey

📋

Stage 1 — Architectural drawings approved

Submit structural & architectural plans to KCCA (Kampala) or district local council. Drawings must be signed by a registered MOWE architect.

Typical: 6–10 weeks
🌿

Stage 2 — NEMA environmental screening

Projects over a threshold size require a NEMA environmental impact assessment or a simpler screening form.

Typical: 3–8 weeks
⚡

Stage 3 — Utility wayleave clearances

UMEME (electricity), NWSC (water/sewer), and UETCL (transmission) wayleave letters. Each is a separate application.

Typical: 2–4 weeks each
🏗️

Stage 4 — Construction commencement permit

Once drawings are approved and fees paid, the commencement permit allows you to break ground. Site hoarding signage required.

Typical: 1–2 weeks after payment
🔒

Stages 5–8 — Inspections, certificate of fitness & title endorsement

Structural inspections at foundation, ring beam and roof stages. Occupation certificate. Final title endorsement. Explained in full in the free guide.
