Verification checklist — before you pay

🔍

Search the title at the Lands Registry

Go to the Ministry of Lands (Lourdel Road, Kampala) or use the e-Lands portal. Confirm the registered owner's name, plot dimensions, and whether any caveats, mortgages, or encumbrances are lodged. Cost: UGX 10,000–20,000.

📐

Commission an independent survey

Hire a licensed surveyor to physically beacon the plot and confirm boundaries match the title plan. Discrepancies often reveal encroachment or a title that covers a different plot entirely.

👨‍💼

Confirm the seller's authority to sell

Ask for national ID and compare it against the registered owner. If the seller is an agent or company director, verify they hold a written Power of Attorney or board resolution — and check it with a lawyer.

🏚️

Check for sitting tenants (Kibanja occupants)

Visit the land on a weekday and speak to anyone farming or living on it. Kibanja holders on Mailo land have statutory rights you must compensate before eviction — ignoring this step can cost more than the land itself.

📜

Review the sale agreement with a conveyancing lawyer

A properly drafted agreement protects your deposit, sets completion timelines, and includes warranties against undisclosed claims. The full guide provides a checklist of the 11 clauses that must be in every Ugandan land sale agreement.
