Uganda landlord obligations checklist

✓

Written tenancy agreement — required for any tenancy over 3 months under the Landlord and Tenant Act 2022.

✓

30-day eviction notice — minimum notice for non-payment of rent; court order needed if tenant refuses to vacate.

✓

Rental Income Tax (RIT) — 30% on net rental income after allowable deductions; must file returns annually with URA.

🔒

Security deposit rules — maximum 2 months, held in trust; deduction rules and how to document condition on entry and exit.

🔒

Service charge transparency — itemised billing required for water, generator, and security levy under 2022 Act s.18.
